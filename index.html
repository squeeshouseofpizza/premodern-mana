Premodern Mana Sources

Paste a decklist. Every spell gets checked against the sources it needs to be cast on curve, and every source gets counted for what it produces in play rather than what its mana symbols say.

Put Sideboard on its own line to separate it. For a card you never hard-cast, write the cost you do pay after the name. A land type counts as its own requirement, so Gush is 4 Gush {Island}{Island} and Snuff Out is 4 Snuff Out {Swamp}.

Paste a decklist on the left.

How to use this

  1. Get your decklist as plain text. On MTGGoldfish, open the deck and use Download or Copy to Clipboard. On MTGTop8, the decklist is already plain text on the page, so select it and copy. In Moxfield it is More, then Export, then Copy for MTGO. Typing it out by hand works too.
  2. Paste it into the box. One card per line with the quantity first, like 4 Wasteland, which is the shape all three of those exports produce. Set codes and collector numbers are ignored, so a line reading 4 Wasteland (TMP) 335 is fine. Lines starting with // are skipped.
  3. Mark the sideboard by putting Sideboard on its own line before it. Sideboard cards are still checked, but they are labelled and they do not drive the headline number for a colour, since you are not casting them in every game.
  4. Correct any card you do not hard-cast. Write the cost you actually pay after the name. 4 Foil {0} for a card you always pitch to. If the real cost is lands rather than mana, name the land type: 4 Gush {Island}{Island}, 4 Snuff Out {Swamp}, 4 Daze {Island}.
  5. Press Analyse. It also runs on its own a moment after you stop typing.

Reading the results

The colour panels
The large number is your source count for that colour. Under it is the largest requirement any maindeck spell puts on that colour, and the card named is the one putting it there. Green clears, red does not. Colours with no sources and no requirement collapse to a single line.
Every spell, worst first
One row per card. Sources reads as have of needed, so 13 of 14 is one short. Reliable from is the turn your existing sources support the card: on curve means the turn it costs, and anything later is given as a turn number. Sorted by shortfall, so the card your mana base fails sits at the top.
turn 4 · short 2
Not castable on curve, but castable from turn four with the sources you already have. The turn is usually the more useful of the two, since a card you hold rather than curve out with is not really failing. Never means no turn inside the tables gets there at this land count.
needs both
One test with a condition per colour. You need both at once, so the worse colour decides the row and the later turn decides when it becomes reliable. Each requirement also goes up by one, since two things have to line up rather than one.
SB · alt cost
Sideboard card, and a card whose cost you overrode on its line.
Sources not counted on turn one
Lands that make a colour but could not have made it on turn one, each with the reason: entered tapped, needs mana paid into it, carries a condition. They count again from turn two, so a card short on turn one is often fine one turn later.
Worth knowing about this list
Facts about this list specifically. What your fetchlands can find in it, whether your conditional lands are switched on, which sources make restricted mana, and anything that changes what your other cards do.

If a colour comes up short

Three options. Add sources of that colour. Accept the later turn, if you hold the card rather than curve out with it. Cut the card, if it wants a mana base the rest of the deck cannot pay for.

Swapping a tapland for an untapped one moves the turn-one number and nothing else. From turn two both count the same.

How your mana is counted

Requirement numbers are Frank Karsten's, from his 2022 update. A spell counts as castable on curve when you have at least an 89 + M percent chance of drawing the coloured sources for it by turn M, conditional on having drawn M lands. One-drops are held to 90 percent, four-drops to 93. He publishes the numbers at 20, 25 and 30 lands; the column here tracks your list and interpolates between them.

Turn one counts untapped sources only. From turn two everything counts, tapped or not, on the reasoning that a tapland could have been played a turn earlier. That one rule is why a card can miss on turn one and clear on turn two without the deck changing.

Multicolour costs are split, looked up per colour, and each requirement raised by one. They report as one row rather than one per colour. You need both at once, so the worse colour decides the row and the later turn decides when it becomes reliable.

Falling short is a statement about timing rather than a failure. One coloured pip wants 14 sources at 25 lands to be reliable on turn one, and 13 by turn two. Anything short is also given the turn your current sources do support it.

Gush is where mana costs stop describing the cost. Free-cast, it wants two Islands in play, and two blue sources is a different quantity: Skycloud Expanse and Tainted Isle both make blue and neither is an Island. Land types written as pips count against lands carrying that type, plus any fetchland that can find one. Snow-Covered Islands carry the type.

There are no dual lands in the format. A fetchland finds a basic, so its colours come from the rest of your list. Flooded Strand in a deck with no Islands is not a blue source.

Mana symbols overstate a lot of lands here. Skycloud Expanse prints white and blue and makes neither alone. Cabal Coffers and Gaea's Cradle make nothing on an empty board. The Tainted cycle is colourless until a Swamp is out. Anything excluded from a count is listed above it with the reason.

Nonland mana is included. Dorks count half, because they die. Artifact accelerants count three quarters. Mox Diamond counts one. Rituals count zero: Dark Ritual multiplies black you already have, and it will not cast a turn-one Duress.

Why a general-purpose calculator gets this format wrong

The arithmetic is portable. The assumption underneath it is not, and the assumption is that a card database can tell you what a card produces.

Look up Reflecting Pool and the data returns white, blue, black, red and green. On an empty board it produces nothing. Cabal Coffers returns black and makes nothing without Swamps. Gaea's Cradle returns green and makes nothing without creatures. Skycloud Expanse returns white and blue and makes neither by itself. Read four Reflecting Pool that way and you have four sources of every colour, which is wrong by four sources of every colour.

Fetchlands are fixing where duals exist. Counting one as a full source of everything it can find is right in a format where it finds a shock land. Here it finds a basic and costs a life, so it is a blue source if and only if you are playing Islands.

Costs are not always mana. Read Gush's mana cost and it is a five-mana blue spell, which is not a thing anyone does with it.

Conditions disappear in a colour lookup. Tainted Field makes colour while a Swamp is out. Temple of the False God is blank below five lands. Neither fact survives the question of what colours a card can make.

And a large share of the mana here is not lands: rituals, Lotus Petal, Mox Diamond, the dorks. Every one of them was read and classified by hand for this page.
