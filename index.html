How your mana is counted

Requirement numbers come from Frank Karsten's 2022 tables. A spell is consistently castable when you have at least an 89 + M percent chance of drawing the coloured sources it needs by turn M, counting only games where you drew enough lands. A one-drop is held to 90 percent, a four-drop to 93. The tables are published at 20, 25 and 30 lands and the column here follows your list, interpolating in between.

On turn one only untapped sources count. From turn two onward everything counts, tapped or not, since a tapland could have been played earlier. Multicolour spells have each colour looked up separately and then every requirement raised by one, because you need both halves at once.

Premodern has no dual lands. A fetchland finds a basic, so its colours are read from the rest of your decklist rather than assumed from the card. Play Flooded Strand without an Island and it is not a blue source here.

A land's mana symbols are not a promise. Skycloud Expanse prints white and blue and produces neither on its own. Cabal Coffers and Gaea's Cradle produce nothing on an empty board. The Tainted cycle is colourless until you control a Swamp. Every land in the format was read individually and classified by hand, and every source excluded from a count here is listed with the reason.

Mana that isn't a land is counted too. Dorks contribute half a source because they die, artifact accelerants three quarters, and Mox Diamond a full one. Rituals and other one-shot effects are deliberately not counted as sources: they multiply mana you already have rather than making a colour available, and Dark Ritual cannot cast your turn-one Duress.
